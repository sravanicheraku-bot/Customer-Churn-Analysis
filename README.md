
# Customer Churn Analysis | Data Analytics Project

## 1. Project Overview

Customer Churn Analysis is a data analytics project focused on understanding why customers leave a company and identifying factors that influence customer retention.

The project uses the Telco Customer Churn dataset to analyze customer behavior, subscription details, contract types, payment methods, and monthly charges.

The main objective is to identify churn patterns and provide business recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 2. Business Problem

The company is experiencing customer churn, which affects revenue and business growth.

The business wants to understand:

- How many customers are leaving the company?
- What is the overall customer churn rate?
- Which contract types have higher churn?
- Which internet services are associated with higher churn?
- Does monthly billing affect customer churn?
- Which customer segments need retention strategies?

## 3. Dataset Information

**Dataset:** Telco Customer Churn

**Source:** IBM / Kaggle Telco Customer Churn dataset

**Key Columns:**
- CustomerID
- Gender
- SeniorCitizen
- Partner
- Dependents
- Tenure
- PhoneService
- InternetService
- Contract
- PaymentMethod
- MonthlyCharges
- TotalCharges
- Churn

## 4. Tools & Technologies

- Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
- DAX – Measures and calculated columns
- Excel – Data inspection and validation

## 5. Data Cleaning & Preparation

The following data preparation steps were performed:

- Checked missing and duplicate values.
- Handled blank values in TotalCharges.
- Corrected data types.
- Verified customer records.
- Created calculated measures for business KPIs.

## 6. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Retained Customers
- Customer Churn Rate
- Average Monthly Charges
- Average Total Charges
- Average Customer Tenure

## 7. Dashboard Features

The interactive Power BI dashboard includes:

- Customer Churn Overview
- Churn by Gender
- Churn by Contract Type
- Churn by Internet Service
- Churn by Payment Method
- Churn by Tenure
- Monthly Charges Analysis

**Interactive Filters:**
- Gender
- Senior Citizen
- Internet Service
- Contract
- Payment Method

## 8. Key Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- Customer segments with higher churn rates.
- Contract types associated with customer attrition.
- Payment methods linked to churn patterns.
- The relationship between customer tenure and churn.
- How monthly charges vary between churned and retained customers.

## 9. Business Recommendations

1. Introduce loyalty benefits for customers with short tenure.
2. Offer attractive long-term contract plans.
3. Provide targeted retention offers to high-risk customers.
4. Review pricing strategies for customers with higher monthly charges.
5. Improve customer engagement and service quality.

## 10. Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how data analytics and Power BI can transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights.

It helps businesses understand customer behavior, identify churn patterns, and make data-driven decisions to improve customer retention.
